Correct AI Usage

Using AI correctly in college means treating it like a tutor.
Students are able to ask AI to explain a hard concept, quiz them before an exam, or check grammar.
The thinking, the writing, and the final answers should still come from the student.
Any time AI helps with an assignment, the student should follow the course policy and cite it.

AI Response:
Your reasoning is sound. Framing AI as a tutor keeps the learning with the student.
One alternative perspective: "correct use" depends on each instructor's policy, so the same action can be
allowed in one course and prohibited in another. Students should also verify AI output, because AI can
state incorrect facts with confidence. Checking sources is part of using AI responsibly.

Benefits of AI

  1. Saves time on repetitive tasks
  2. Explains difficult concepts in simple terms

Who Benefits?

Incorrect AI Usage

Submitting AI Work as Your Own
Turning in an essay that AI wrote without permission or citation.

Using AI During Exams
Asking AI for answers on a quiz or test when outside help is not allowed.

Making Up Sources
Copying AI citations into a paper without checking that the sources are real.

In my opinion, AI is a powerful tool that can make learning easier. The problem starts when students use it to replace their own thinking. Using AI to cheat hurts the student most because they never learn the skills they will need at work. Aggies don't lie, cheat, or steal, and that includes with AI.
