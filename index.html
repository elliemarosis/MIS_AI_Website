This website explores Management Information Systems (MIS) and the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in college and the workplace. MIS brings people, technology, and business processes together so organizations can make better decisions. AI is now a big part of MIS because it can sort through huge amounts of data and find patterns that people might miss. Groups like MIT Technology Review report on how fast these tools are changing business every year. My pages also cover cybersecurity, since protecting information is one of the most important jobs of any information system. You can learn more about the field from the Association for Information Systems, and we hope our site helps you use AI the right way.

Management Information Systems (MIS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) are transforming how organizations collect, manage, and use information. MIS integrates people, technology, data, and business processes to support effective decision-making. AI strengthens MIS by analyzing large data sets, recognizing patterns, and producing valuable insights, a trend MIT Technology Review follows closely. Because information is only useful when it is protected, this website also examines malware, hackers, and cybersecurity. Most importantly, it explains the ethical boundaries of using AI in academia and the workplace. Professional groups such as the Association for Information Systems show how these fields continue to grow together.
