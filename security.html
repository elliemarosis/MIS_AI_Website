Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware spread across the world in just a few days. It encrypted files on more than 200,000 computers in about 150 countries and demanded a Bitcoin payment to unlock them. The attack shut down parts of the United Kingdom's National Health Service, forcing hospitals to cancel appointments and turn away patients. WannaCry spread by using a Windows flaw called EternalBlue, even though Microsoft had already released a patch for it. It was significant in cybersecurity history because it showed how dangerous unpatched systems are and how one piece of malware can disrupt critical services worldwide.
